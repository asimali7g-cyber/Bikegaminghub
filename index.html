Best bike games for Android

Our ranked list. Always check the Google Play page for the latest version, price and size.

  1. Traffic Rider

    Best overall. Weave through highway traffic in first-person view. Simple controls and plenty of bikes to unlock.

  2. Moto Rider GO: Highway Traffic

    Great for short sessions. Easy to start, with many missions and bike upgrades.

  3. Trial Xtreme 4

    Best for skill. Balance your bike over ramps and obstacles. Hard, but very satisfying.

  4. Moto X3M

    Best for quick fun. Fast stunt levels with flips and tricks that suit small screens.

  5. Mad Skills Motocross 3

    Best dirt-bike racing. Online and offline races with bike tuning.

  6. MotoGP Racing (official game)

    Best for real racing fans. Official-style racing for people who love road racing.

How to choose bike games for a low-end Android phone

Many people think they need an expensive phone to play bike games. That is not true. A few simple checks will help you pick games that run smoothly.

  • Check the file size. Smaller games usually need less power.
  • Look at the reviews. Search for words like "lag" or "crash".
  • Lower the graphics. Most games have a Low or Medium setting.
  • Free up storage. A full phone runs slowly.

7 tips to win more bike races

  1. Learn the track. Play it twice before you push hard.
  2. Brake early before sharp turns.
  3. Upgrade your engine first, then your brakes.
  4. Use boosts on straight roads, not in corners.
  5. In trial games, move slowly and stay calm.
  6. Turn on tilt or button controls, whichever feels better.
  7. Take breaks. Tired players make mistakes.

Are bike games safe to download?

Yes, if you use the official Google Play Store. Avoid websites that offer "free modded" files, because they can contain viruses or steal your data. Always read reviews and check the developer name.

Bike games without internet

Many bike games work offline, especially stunt and highway games. Check the Google Play description for the words "offline" or "no Wi-Fi needed", then test the game by turning on airplane mode.
